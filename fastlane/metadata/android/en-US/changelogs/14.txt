Mostly cosmetic changes with v4.0 non-safe mode ("Strict mode") promoted from experimental to stable after extensive dogfooding.

• Safe mode switch is replaced by a Behavior choice: Conservative (default) or Strict.
• "Permission mode" is renamed to "Access provider".
• Deleting a schedule now asks for confirmation.
• Fixed clipped or hidden content in short, wide and landscape windows and with large text.
• Added hint to touch grass (see if you can find it).
