<b>Features</b>
• <b>Flexible schedules.</b> One-off or repeating on the days you choose.
• <b>Overnight schedules.</b> Schedules can carry over to the next day.
• <b>Full control.</b> Skip, postpone, pause or end from the notification.
• <b>Wi-Fi control.</b> Off when a schedule starts, back on when it ends.
• <b>Safe by default.</b> Ending a schedule undoes only what it changed; anything you change yourself stays.
• <b>Notifications.</b> Quiet notifications before, during and after a schedule.
• <b>No internet access, ads or tracking.</b>

<b>Note:</b> This app needs Shizuku, or Sui on a rooted phone.
