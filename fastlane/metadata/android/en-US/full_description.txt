<b>Features</b>
• <b>Flexible schedules.</b> Run once or repeat on the days you choose.
• <b>Overnight schedules.</b> A schedule can continue into the next day.
• <b>Full control.</b> Skip, postpone, pause or end a schedule at any time.
• <b>Wi-Fi control.</b> Turn off Wi-Fi when a schedule starts, and turn it back on when it ends.
• <b>Safe by default.</b> Ending a schedule only reverts changes the app made.
• <b>Notifications.</b> Quiet notifications before, during and after a schedule.
• <b>No internet access, ads or tracking ever.</b>

<b>Requirements</b>
• Android 9 or later.
• <b>Non-root:</b> <a href="https://github.com/rushiranpise/Shizuku-Next">Shizuku-Next</a> (recommended) or <a href="https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku">Shizuku</a>.
• <b>Root:</b> <a href="https://github.com/topjohnwu/Magisk">Magisk</a> or another root access manager, <a href="https://github.com/RikkaApps/Sui">Sui</a>, or Shizuku/Shizuku-Next as root.
